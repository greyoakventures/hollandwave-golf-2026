Event Canceled

Thank You — See You in 2027

As of Friday, July 31, 2026, our team-registration deadline has passed and we did not reach the minimum number of teams required to run this year’s outing. After a lot of prayer and conversation with the committee, we’ve made the difficult decision to cancel the 2026 Holland Wave Golf Outing.

We know a lot of people were pulling for this event — golfers, sponsors, families, and volunteers alike. To everyone who committed early, promoted it in your circles, or was praying about joining: thank you. Your support means more to this program than a single fundraising day.

What happens next: Any golfers or sponsors who have already paid will be contacted directly by the committee about refunds. If you’ve already sent in a check or online payment and don’t hear from us within the next two weeks, please reach out using the info below.

Looking ahead: We are already working to bring the golf outing back in 2027 — with more lead time, earlier sponsor outreach, and a stronger push in the homeschool community. If you’d like to be on the early-notice list for next year, just email us and we’ll add you.

Holland Wave homeschool basketball — boys and girls — continues on. Please keep our players, coaches, and families in your prayers as we head into the 2026–27 season.

Why We Play

Building the next generation
