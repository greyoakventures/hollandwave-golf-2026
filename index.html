Why We Play

Building the next generation

Holland Wave has served West Michigan homeschool families for 10+ years, giving Christian homeschool students a place to grow as athletes, teammates, and disciples.

Boys teams have run since 2014, and we're bringing girls basketball back for the 2026 season. Every dollar raised lowers athlete fees and funds coaches, uniforms, and tournament travel.

This one day funds a full year of homeschool basketball for boys and girls.
